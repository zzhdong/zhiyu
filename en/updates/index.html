RELEASE NOTES / 2026.10.28

Tools that keep getting better.

Zhiyu is now available on the App Store. These are the notes for version 2026.10.28; check your local storefront for the latest available version.

VERSION 2026.10.28

What's new in 2026.10.28

Six new tools and features in this update:

  • ✓Bulk QR code generation: enter multiple lines or import CSV/TXT files, generate up to 100 codes per batch, and export PNG/SVG files in a ZIP.
  • ✓Image upscaling and SVG optimisation: enlarge images by 2× or 4× using interpolation with optional light sharpening, and remove unnecessary SVG comments, editor metadata and whitespace.
  • ✓Time zone and timestamp conversion: convert dates across time zones and work with Unix timestamps in seconds, milliseconds or microseconds.
  • ✓Cron expression builder and parser: create and parse common five- and six-field expressions, with previews of upcoming run times. Some advanced syntax is not supported.
  • ✓Thermal-style camera effects: apply false-colour effects to an ordinary camera image. This is not infrared thermal imaging and cannot measure temperature.
  • ✓Regular expression workbench: use presets, inspect matches and preview text replacements.

We have also refined the iPad workspace, including its sidebar, landscape and portrait layouts, and tool navigation. Calendar and Reminders on iPad have improved holiday displays and editing flows. Fixes include tapping blank areas of buttons, retaining filters when navigating back, and better stability across different screens and window sizes.

Noise and heart-rate estimates do not replace medical equipment or a professional sound level meter.
