HOW IT WORKS

Process diagram, not an app screenshot
  1. 01

    Read the notice

    This is not a medical device or diagnostic test.

  2. 02

    Place a fingertip

    Cover the rear camera and flash area as instructed.

  3. 03

    Hold still

    Allow enough usable samples; avoid movement and excessive pressure.

  4. 04

    Review BPM

    Use the result as an estimate and follow any measurement error guidance.

Before you begin

Does not access Apple Health or keep a medical history. It cannot measure blood pressure, blood oxygen, glucose or temperature.
