Scrolling Screenshot

Stitch long screenshots from authorised recording, images or videos.

←Back to all guides20 guide topics

HOW IT WORKS

Process diagram, not an app screenshot
  1. 01

    Choose a source

    Use an explicit screen recording or import local images or a video.

  2. 02

    Start capture

    Cross-app capture uses Apple's ReplayKit broadcast permission. Scroll and stop as instructed.

  3. 03

    Preview the stitch

    Check on-device results and any quality warnings; very fast scrolling may reduce matching quality.

  4. 04

    Export

    Inspect the result and export PNG or PDF as needed.

Before you begin

Screen content is captured only with your authorisation. No audio is recorded and your screen contents are not uploaded. Results depend on the device and source material.
