USER GUIDE / BULK_QR

Bulk QR code generation

Create and export a batch of QR codes.

←Back to all guides28 guide topics

HOW IT WORKS

Process diagram, not an app screenshot
  1. 01

    Open batch generation

    Choose the bulk tool in QR Workbench.

  2. 02

    Enter or import

    Provide one item per line, or import CSV/TXT.

  3. 03

    Generate and review

    Create up to 100 codes per batch and review their contents.

  4. 04

    Export and share

    Export PNG/SVG files and share them as a ZIP archive.

Before you begin

Maximum 100 items per batch. Review content and exports before sharing; scanned URLs never open automatically.
