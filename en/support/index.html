Support

Need help? Contact the developer.

Zhiyu is maintained by an independent developer. When reporting an issue, include the app version, device model, iOS/iPadOS version and steps to reproduce it. Please do not include precise locations, calendar contents or other unrelated sensitive information.

Weather

Location and weather

Location access is requested only when you choose to use your current location. If weather does not update, check your network connection, iOS location permissions and the data-source status on the Weather screen.

Calendar

Calendar and reminders

iOS asks separately for calendar and notification access. You can review those permissions under Settings → Privacy & Security and Settings → Notifications.

Device tests

Device checks

Camera, microphone, motion, location and Face ID are used only when you actively start the relevant check. The corresponding process stops when you leave the test.

Measurement

Measurement tools

Noise estimates require microphone access. Low-light enhancement and heart-rate estimates require camera access; Heart Rate Test also uses the rear flash. Heart-rate estimates are not for medical diagnosis. Noise estimates are not calibrated for your device and cannot replace a professional sound level meter.

Widgets

Home Screen widgets

When iOS allows a widget timeline refresh, the Weather widget may update MET Norway weather using a saved area-level location without requesting background location. iOS controls the actual refresh timing. If data remains outdated, open Zhiyu and check the connection and Weather data sources.

Purchase

Purchase, billing and refunds

Zhiyu is a one-time paid download, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases in the current version. Apple handles App Store billing and refund requests.
